About Private Drive Expo

Private Drive Expo (Pty) Ltd is a South African private transport company focused on providing professional, reliable and comfortable transportation services.

Our goal is to make every journey safe, convenient and stress-free. Whether you are travelling to the airport, attending a business meeting, taking a family trip or travelling long distance, we are ready to assist.

We also provide opportunities for drivers interested in e-hailing and professional driving services.
